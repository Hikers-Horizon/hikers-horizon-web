📋 Step-by-Step User Guide
Simple Setup
1
Get License Key & Activate
Visit swiftseat.shop, enter your email and complete ₹99 payment. Copy your license key and activate it in the extension/dashboard.
2
Configure Form & Passenger Details
Fill in IRCTC login credentials, stations (e.g. YNK to YG), train number, travel class, journey date, and list of passengers into the form.
3
Start Automation or Enable Auto-Start
Click Start Sequence or enable Auto-Start mode (launches 30 seconds before 10:00 AM / 11:00 AM Tatkal window).
4
Select Class & Click Book Now
The bot navigates to train results. Click your preferred class (Sleeper / AC) and click Book Now. The bot instantly injects all passenger details!
5
Type Payment CAPTCHA & Approve UPI
Type the CAPTCHA code into the box, choose your UPI payment method (GPay/PhonePe/Paytm), and approve the transaction on your smartphone.
💡
Pro-Tip for Fast Payment
Open your UPI app (Google Pay, PhonePe, or Paytm) on your smartphone before starting. The moment you hit Submit on IRCTC, approve the payment notification immediately!
❓ Frequently Asked Questions
Support
Are my login credentials safe?
Yes. Credentials and passenger details are saved only on your local browser. No data is stored on remote servers.
Which browsers are supported?
Google Chrome and Microsoft Edge on Windows, macOS, and Linux desktop operating systems.
What if IRCTC site slows down?
If the website hangs, press F5 (Reload). The bot will automatically resume from the step you were on.
How long does the license last?
Your ₹99 license key gives 30 days of active subscription with unlimited Tatkal ticket bookings.
