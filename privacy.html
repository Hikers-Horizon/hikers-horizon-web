Privacy Policy for Swift Seat Mobile Bot

Last Updated: July 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Swift Seat Mobile Bot ("we", "our", or "us") handles user information when you use our Chrome Extension.

1. Local Data Storage

Swift Seat Mobile Bot stores all user-entered booking configuration details—including station codes, train numbers, journey dates, passenger details, and license keys—strictly locally on your device using the browser's chrome.storage.local API.

2. Data Collection and Transmission

We do not collect, track, sell, or transmit your personal data, credentials, browsing history, or payment information to external servers. The extension only sends HTTPS REST API requests to https://hikershorizon.in/api/activate-license to validate user-purchased subscription keys and device registration IDs.

3. Use of Permissions

  • storage: Used solely to save your booking configuration locally so you do not need to re-enter details repeatedly.
  • activeTab: Used exclusively to inject passenger details into supported booking forms (irctc.co.in and aranyavihaara.karnataka.gov.in) when you initiate automation.
  • host_permissions: Used strictly to verify subscription license keys via our secure API endpoint.

4. Data Security

Your authentication credentials and passenger preferences remain contained within your local browser environment. We implement industry-standard encryption practices for license validation calls.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted directly on this page.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@hikershorizon.in.
