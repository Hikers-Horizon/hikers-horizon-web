Step-by-Step Setup Guide
Easy 5 Steps
1
Get License Key & Activate
Visit hikershorizon.in/swiftseat, enter your email and pay ₹99. Copy your generated key and paste it into the dashboard/extension to activate.
2
Configure Form Details
Enter your IRCTC login credentials, route (e.g., YNK to YG), train number, class, journey date, and passenger list into the dashboard.
3
Start Sequence or Set Auto-Start
Click Start Sequence or enable Auto-Start mode (starts 30s before 10 AM / 11 AM window). The bot opens IRCTC and initiates search.
4
Select Class & Book Now (Manual Step)
The bot navigates to train results. Immediately click your class (Sleeper/AC) and click Book Now. The bot instantly injects all passengers!
5
Solve Captcha & Complete Payment
Type the Captcha, select your preferred payment mode (UPI/Card), and approve payment on your phone to confirm your ticket!
💡
Pro-Tip for Maximum Speed
Keep your UPI app (GPay / PhonePe / Paytm) open on your smartphone before starting. Once you submit the payment step on IRCTC, approve the UPI request immediately!
Frequently Asked Questions
FAQ
Are my credentials safe?
Yes. Password & passenger data are stored strictly on your local device. We never transmit or store your login info on external servers.
Which browsers are supported?
Google Chrome and Microsoft Edge on Windows, macOS, and Linux desktop operating systems.
What if the page hangs?
If IRCTC servers slow down, simply press F5 to reload. The bot resumes automation automatically from the current step.
How long does ₹99 subscription last?
Your license key remains active for 30 days with unlimited ticket bookings during the subscription period.
