Getting Started
How to Use Swift Seat
1

Install the Extension

Download Swift Seat from the Chrome Web Store or Microsoft Edge Add-ons. Click "Add to browser" and pin the extension icon.

2

Get Your License Key (₹99/month)

Visit hikershorizon.in/swiftseat → scroll to the "SaaS Licensing Portal" card → enter your email → pay ₹99 via UPI/Card → copy your license key.

3

Activate Your Key

Click the Swift Seat extension icon → paste your license key → click "Activate". The extension is now linked to your browser.

4

Configure Your Journey

Open the dashboard and enter: IRCTC username/password, From & To stations, Train number, Class, Date, Quota, and Passenger details.

5

Start the Automation

Click "START SEQUENCE" or set Auto-Start to trigger before the Tatkal window. The bot handles login, search, train selection, and passenger filling automatically.

6

Complete Payment

You only need to type the CAPTCHA and complete payment on IRCTC. Swift Seat does everything else for you.

💡 Pro Tip
For the best Tatkal success rate, set Auto-Start mode to trigger 30 seconds before the window opens. Keep your browser open and connected to the internet. The bot will take over automatically.
⚠️ Important Note
Swift Seat automates form-filling only. You must have a valid IRCTC account and complete the CAPTCHA and payment manually. We do not store your payment or banking details.
FAQ
Common Questions
Is my IRCTC password safe?
Yes. Your credentials are stored locally in your browser's encrypted storage. They are never sent to our servers.
Can I use it on multiple devices?
Each license key is tied to one browser/device for security. Contact support if you need to transfer your license.
What happens after 30 days?
Your subscription expires and the extension stops working. Simply renew for another ₹99 to continue using Swift Seat.
Which browsers are supported?
Google Chrome and Microsoft Edge. We recommend using Edge for the best compatibility with IRCTC.
