Introduction

Welcome to Hikers Horizon. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This policy outlines how we handle your information.

Last updated: May 14, 2025

1. Information We Collect

  • Personal Identification: Name, email address, phone number
  • Trekking Preferences: Skill level, medical requirements, gear sizes
  • Payment Data: Handled securely through encrypted payment gateways (Razorpay)
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, and usage patterns

2. How We Use Data

  • To process and manage your trek bookings
  • To send automated email receipts and travel updates
  • To coordinate safety and logistics during treks
  • To improve our platform and user experience

3. Data Security

  • We use state-of-the-art encryption for data storage
  • Strict access controls for administrative personnel
  • Regular security audits of our server infrastructure
  • Secure Socket Layer (SSL) technology for all transactions

4. Your Rights

  • Right to access your personal information at any time
  • Right to request deletion of your account and data
  • Right to opt-out of marketing communications
  • Right to correct any inaccuracies in your profile
