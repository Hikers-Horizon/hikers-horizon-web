About the Gokarna Beach Trek

The legendary Gokarna Beach Trek from Bangalore is India's premier coastal trekking experience, offering the perfect blend of raw adventure, serene beaches, and tropical vibes. Organized by the best gokarna trek organizer, Hikers Horizon, this 2-day weekend getaway takes you along the rugged, cliff-lined coastline of the Arabian Sea in Karnataka. Known as the coastal trek of Karnataka, this experience is a must-do for anyone looking to escape the hustle of the city.

Our journey covers the famous five beaches of Gokarna. We start from Belekan Beach and traverse the scenic rocky cliffs to reach the secluded paradise of Paradise Beach, followed by the half-moon-shaped shores of Half Moon Beach. The adventure continues as we trek to the iconic Om Beach—famous for its natural 'Om' shape—and wind down at the sunset-viewing haven of Kudle Beach. Finally, we finish our coastal trail at Gokarna Beach, exploring local markets and culture.

Unlike typical weekend trips, our premium Gokarna beach camping trek includes comfortable beachfront tent stays, dynamic campfire sessions, and all-inclusive transportation. In addition to the beach trail, we visit the historic Mirjan Fort, the towering Shiva statue at Murudeshwar Temple, and experience a peaceful boating session in the scenic Honnavara Backwaters. Gokarna Trek Itinerary

Day 0 Departure from Bengaluru

As the sun sets over Bangalore, our adventure begins. We'll embark on a comfortable journey towards Gokarna in a Tempo Traveler or Minibus with push-back seats.

Day 1 Temple and Beaches!

• 09:00 AM: Arrival at Campsite and Get Ready
• 10:30 AM: Depart to Belekan Beach, the starting point of our trek
• 11:00 AM: Trek to Paradise Beach (30 mins) & Enjoy the secluded cove
• 12:30 PM: Trek to Half-Moon Beach (1 hour) & Relax
• 02:30 PM: Trek to Om Beach (1 hour) & Lunch (Self-Sponsored)
• 04:30 PM: Trek through the Forest to Kudle Beach for a mesmerizing sunset
• 06:30 PM: Trek to Gokarna Beach stay while exploring the Gokarna Market
• 09:30 PM: Campfire, Dinner and retire for the night

Day 2 Fort, Temple and Backwaters!

• 06:00 AM: Wake up and Breakfast
• 08:30 AM: Check-out and head to Mirjan Fort
• 09:30 AM: Explore the majestic Mirjan Fort
• 11:00 AM: Visit Murudeshwar Temple & the giant Shiva Statue
• 01:00 PM: Lunch (Self-Sponsored)
• 03:00 PM: Honnavara Backwaters Boating experience (Self-Sponsored)
• 06:00 PM: Depart for Bengaluru with dinner en route

Day 3 Homeward Bound

05:00 AM: Reach Bangalore. Drop-offs at the same points as pick-ups. Time to head home with memories of a lifetime!

Gokarna Trek Guidelines

  • • The trek distance is approximately 10 km (one side).
  • • Alcohol consumption is strictly prohibited during the trip.
  • • Stay is provided in tent stays/beachside cottages.
  • • Murudeshwar requires modest clothing (Temple dress code applies).
  • • Carry a xerox copy of your Aadhar/Voter ID (Mandatory for permits).
