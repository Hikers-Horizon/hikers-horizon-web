The Coastal Expedition

Adventure on the Arabian Shore

The legendary Gokarna Beach Trek from Bangalore is India's most iconic coastal trekking trail. Organized by Hikers Horizon, this 2-day weekend expedition takes you along the rugged, cliff-lined coastline of the Arabian Sea in Karnataka.

Our journey takes you through the spiritual and coastal charm of Gokarna: visiting the revered Mahabaleshwar Temple, soaking in the coastal vibes at Om Beach, catching a mesmerizing golden sunset at Kudle Beach, and trekking to our beachside camp through the lively town market. On Day 2, explore the majestic 16th-century laterite ramparts of Mirjan Fort, visit the grand sea-facing Murudeshwar Temple, and enjoy scenic backwater boating in Honnavara before returning comfortably to Bengaluru.

🏛️

Cultural Heritage & Temple Dress Code

Visits to Mahabaleshwar Temple (Gokarna) and Murudeshwar Temple require traditional attire. Men must wear dhotis or mundus; women are required to wear sarees or salwar kameez covering shoulders and knees. Shorts and western revealing attire are strictly prohibited inside the sanctum.

Common Queries

Frequently Asked Questions

Can beginners do the Gokarna beach trek?

Yes, absolutely! The coastal trail is classified as easy to moderate. There are some minor rocky patches between beaches, but anyone with basic fitness can complete it comfortably.

Is swimming allowed at the beaches?

Swimming is permitted at designated gentle spots on Kudle Beach and Om Beach under trek leader supervision. Avoid deep water at Paradise and Belekan during high tides.

What is the dress code for Mahabaleshwar and Murudeshwar temples?

Traditional attire is mandatory. Men should wear dhotis or mundus (or pants with covered shoulders); women must wear sarees or salwar suits that cover shoulders and knees. Shorts or sleeveless shirts are not allowed inside sanctum areas.
