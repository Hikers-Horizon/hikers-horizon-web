About the Gokarna Beach Trek

The legendary Gokarna Beach Trek from Bangalore is India's premier coastal trekking experience, offering the perfect blend of raw adventure, serene beaches, and tropical vibes. Organized by the best gokarna trek organizer, Hikers Horizon, this 2-day weekend getaway takes you along the rugged, cliff-lined coastline of the Arabian Sea in Karnataka. Known as the coastal trek of Karnataka, this experience is a must-do for anyone looking to escape the hustle of the city.

Our journey covers the famous five beaches of Gokarna. We start from Belekan Beach and traverse the scenic rocky cliffs to reach the secluded paradise of Paradise Beach, followed by the half-moon-shaped shores of Half Moon Beach. The adventure continues as we trek to the iconic Om Beach—famous for its natural 'Om' shape—and wind down at the sunset-viewing haven of Kudle Beach. Finally, we finish our coastal trail at Gokarna Beach, exploring local markets and culture.

Unlike typical weekend trips, our premium Gokarna beach camping trek includes comfortable beachfront tent stays, dynamic campfire sessions, and all-inclusive transportation. In addition to the beach trail, we visit the historic Mirjan Fort, the towering Shiva statue at Murudeshwar Temple, and experience a peaceful boating session in the scenic Honnavara Backwaters. Day 1 Embrace the Natural Beauty of Gokarna’s Beaches

• 9:00 AM – 10:00 AM: Arrival at Campsite and Get Ready
• 10:00 AM – 10:30 AM: Breakfast and Campsite Exploration
• 10:30 AM – 11:00 AM: Depart to and reach Belekan Beach, the starting point of our trek
• 11:00 AM – 11:30 AM: Trek from Belekan Beach to Paradise Beach (30 mins)
• 11:30 AM – 12:30 PM: Enjoy Paradise Beach
• 12:30 PM – 01:30 PM: Trek to and reach Half-Moon Beach (1 hour)
• 1:30 PM – 02:30 PM: Relax at Half-Moon Beach
• 2:30 PM – 03:30 PM: Trek to and reach Om Beach (1 hour)
• 3:30 PM – 04:00 PM: Enjoy Om Beach
• 4:00 PM – 04:30 PM: Lunch*Self-Sponsored
• 4:30 PM – 05:00 PM: Trek through the Forest to Kudle Beach (30 mins)
• 05:00 PM – 06:30 PM: Sunset at Kudle Beach - Arrive at Kudle Beach in time to catch a mesmerizing sunset.
• 6:30 PM – 08:30 PM: Trek to reach the stay on Gokarna Beach while simultaneously exploring the Gokarna Market (2 hours)
Note: Please take a private transport to reach the stay if you are unable to trek back to the Gokarna Beach, which would take around 1.5 hours to reach.
• 08:30 PM: Head back to the campsite and unwind.
• 09:30 PM - 10:30 PM: Campfire and Dinner - Return to campsite, enjoy the warmth of a campfire, share stories with fellow travelers, have dinner, and then retire for the night.

Day 2 From Cascading Falls to Historic Forts

Day 2: From Cascading Falls to Historic Forts
• 5:00 a.m. – Departure for Vibhooti Falls: Early departure to the enchanting Vibhooti Falls.
• 10:30 a.m. – Explore Vibhooti Falls: Enjoy the serene ambiance and optional swimming.
• 12:00 p.m. – Journey to Yana Caves: Visit the mystical Yana Caves, known for their unique rock formations.
• 2:00 p.m. – Lunch at a Local Restaurant (Self-sponsored): Enjoy local cuisine.
• 3:00 p.m. – Visit Mirjan Fort: Explore the historical architecture of Mirjan Fort.
• 5:30 p.m. – Visit Mahabaleshwar Temple: Experience spiritual moments at the Gokarna Temple.
* NOTE: Dress Code is generally Traditional Attire for Mahabaleshwar Temple i.e. For Men - Dhotis or Mundus & for Women - sarees, salwar kameez, or other traditional outfits that cover their shoulders and knees. Clothing like jeans, shorts, and revealing attire are not permitted.
• 7:00 p.m. – Return and Rest: Return to your accommodation, have dinner and rest for the night.
