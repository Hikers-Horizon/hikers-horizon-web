Conquer the horseback-shaped peak. A majestic climb through Shola forests and mist.

Kudremukh Trek Overview

Difficulty Moderate - Hard
Altitude 6,214 ft.
Duration 2 Days / 1 Night
Region Chikmagalur

Kudremukh, meaning "Horse-Face" in Kannada, is a mountain range and name of the peak located in Chikkamagaluru district. It is the third highest peak in Karnataka. The trek is famous for its rolling green hills, dense shola forests, and the characteristic mist that rolls over the meadows.

Explore the Kudremukh National Park with Karnataka Tourism.

Kudremukh Trek Guidelines

  • • Leeches are common; carry salt or repellent
  • • Zero-plastic zone; please do not carry disposable plastics
  • • Weather changes rapidly; always carry a light jacket and raincoat
  • • Mobile network is sparse; inform family in advance
  • • Shared jeep transport will be used to reach the base camp
