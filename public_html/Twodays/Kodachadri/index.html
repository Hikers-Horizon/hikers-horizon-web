Kodachadri Peak Trek

Venture into the Jasmine of the Ghats. A mystic trail to Sarvajna Peetha and Hidlumane Falls.

Kodachadri Trek Overview

Difficulty Moderate - Hard
Altitude 4,411 ft.
Duration 2 Days / 1 Night
USP Hidlumane Falls

Kodachadri is a natural heritage site and the state's 10th highest peak. The trek is legendary for its diversity—passing through the multi-tiered Hidlumane Falls, dense canopy forests, and finally the ridge-walk to the Sarvajna Peetha where Adi Shankaracharya is said to have meditated. The sunset over the Arabian Sea from the peak is a spiritual experience.

Learn about the Mookambika Wildlife Sanctuary with Karnataka Tourism.

Day 1 The Peak, the Waterfalls and the Glory
  • 06:00 AM - 09:00 AM: Reach Homestay, freshen up, have breakfast, and explore the campsite

Trail Guidelines: Protect Nature & Stay Safe

  • 🚫
    No Smoking – Prevent wildfires and keep the air clean. Smoking is strictly prohibited on the trail.
  • 🔥
    No Campfires – Help protect the environment. Open flames can cause forest fires and damage wildlife habitats.
  • 🚯
    No Plastics – Keep the trail clean and plastic-free. Carry reusable items and pack out all waste. Respect nature, leave no trace, and ensure a safe experience for all! 🌿✨
  • 🍾
    No Alcohol – Stay Safe, Stay Sharp. Consumption of alcohol is strictly prohibited during the trek and at the homestay. Let's keep the vibe clean and the adventure safe!
