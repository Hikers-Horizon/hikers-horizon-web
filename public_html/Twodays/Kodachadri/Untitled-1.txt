# Gokarna Beach Trek with Hikers Horizon!
Looking for a perfect mix of beach hopping, scenic landscapes and cultural discovery? The Gokarna Beach Trek Adventure Trip is a 2-day coastal journey through Karnataka’s serene shoreline. From hidden beaches and ocean treks to majestic temples and backwater escapes – this trip with Hikers Horizon blends adventure and calm in one unforgettable weekend.

## Trip Highlights:
Destination: Gokarna – a tranquil coastal town known for its sacred temples, beach treks and laid-back vibe.Duration: 2 DaysTrip Type: Coastal trekking, sightseeing and cultural exploration – perfect for beach lovers, explorers and weekend wanderers.Best Time to Visit: October to MayLocation: Uttara Kannada District, KarnatakaTravel Time: Approximately 9–10 hours from Bangalore.

## Itinerary Overview:
Day 0: Late night departure from Bangalore (around 08:00 PM). Overnight journey. Day 1: Trek through Belekan Beach, Paradise Beach, Half Moon Beach, Om Beach & Kudle Beach Day 2: Mirjan Fort, Murudeshwar Shiva Statue & Temple, Honnavara Backwaters
NOTE: Upon Confirmation of the event, you will be added to the whatsapp group on the day of departure and further details will be updated on the group itself.

## What You’ll Experience:
🏖️ Beach Hopping: From secluded coves to iconic shores like Om & Kudle🧘‍♂️ Chill Vibes: Relax, unwind, and enjoy a slow-paced beach escape🛕 Spiritual Sites: Marvel at the towering Shiva statue at Murudeshwar🚣‍♀️ Backwater Calm: Discover the quiet beauty of Honnavara’s hidden waters

## Important Information:
📱 Group Coordination: WhatsApp group created on the day of departure for updates and assistance.📞 Contact: For queries, call or WhatsApp +91 991 662 7799.⚠️ Beach Safety: Wear comfortable shoes for trekking and stay hydrated.🚫 No Alcohol: Alcohol consumption is strictly prohibited during the trip.

## Day 1 - Temple and Beaches!
▪️ 9:00 AM – 10:00 AM: Arrival at Campsite and Get Ready
▪️ 10:00 AM – 10:30 AM: Breakfast and Campsite Exploration
▪️ 10:30 AM – 11:00 AM: Depart to and reach Belekan Beach, the starting point of our trek
▪️ 11:00 AM – 11:30 AM: Trek from Belekan Beach to Paradise Beach (30 mins)
▪️ 11:30 AM – 12:30 PM: Enjoy Paradise Beach
▪️ 12:30 PM – 01:30 PM: Trek to and reach Half-Moon Beach (1 hour)
▪️ 1:30 PM – 02:30 PM: Relax at Half-Moon Beach
▪️ 2:30 PM – 03:30 PM: Trek to and reach Om Beach (1 hour)
▪️ 3:30 PM – 04:00 PM: Enjoy Om Beach
▪️ 4:00 PM – 04:30 PM: Lunch*Self-Sponsored
▪️ 4:30 PM – 05:00 PM: Trek through the Forest to Kudle Beach (30 mins)
▪️05:00 PM - 06:30 PM: Sunset at Kudle Beach - Arrive at Kudle Beach in time to catch a mesmerizing sunset.
▪️ 6:30 PM – 08:30 PM: Trek to reach the stay on Gokarna Beach while simultaneously exploring the Gokarna Market (2 hours)
Note: Please take a private transport to reach the stay if you are unable to trek back to the Gokarna Beach, which would take around 1.5 hours to reach.
▪️ 08:30 PM: Head back to the campsite and unwind.
▪️09:30 PM - 10:30 PM: Campfire and Dinner - Return to campsite, enjoy the warmth of a campfire, share stories with fellow travelers, have dinner, and then retire for the night.

▪️  11:00 AM - 01:00 PM: Visit Murudeshwar Temple - Travel to Murudeshwar to visit the renowned temple. Return by noon.
