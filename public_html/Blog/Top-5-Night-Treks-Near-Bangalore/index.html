Top 5 Sunrise Night Treks Near Bangalore

By Hikers Horizon Team • May 2026 • 5 min read

Bangalore's traffic and IT hustle can get exhausting. Luckily, the city is surrounded by ancient hills, fort ruins, and granite monoliths that offer the perfect weekend escape. But why trek in the scorching sun when you can hike under the moonlight and wake up above the clouds?

Here are the top 5 sunrise night treks near Bangalore that you need to experience in 2026.

1. Skandagiri Night Trek

Skandagiri, also known as Kalavara Durga, is arguably the most famous night trek from Bangalore. Located just 60 km away, the trail takes you through dense vegetation in pitch darkness to the ruins of an 18th-century fort.

The Highlight: The famous "ocean of clouds." If you time it right during the winter months, you will literally stand above a blanket of clouds as the sun rises.

2. Savandurga Monolith Trek

Savandurga is Asia's largest granite monolith. Located about 50 km west of Bangalore, this trek is completely different from Skandagiri. Instead of a muddy trail, you are scaling a steep, smooth rock face.

The Highlight: The sheer thrill of the incline and the panoramic views of the Manchanabele Dam at dawn.

3. Makalidurga Sunrise Trek

Makalidurga offers a beautiful blend of history and nature. Located near Doddaballapur, the trek begins near a railway track and winds up a hill crowned with an old fort and a Shiva temple.

The Highlight: Watching the sunrise illuminate the train tracks and the lake at the base of the hill.

4. Uttari Betta (Hutridurga)

Uttari Betta is a 5km trek near Kunigal that takes you through seven doors of stone to reach the top. It's relatively easy, making it perfect for beginners who still want the thrill of a night trek.

The Highlight: The beautiful sunrise view from the ancient temple at the summit.

5. Kunti Betta Night Trek

Located in the historic town of Pandavapura, Kunti Betta has mythological significance tying back to the Mahabharata. It's a rocky terrain with some steep sections that require scrambling.

The Highlight: The view of the backwaters of the Thonnur lake shimmering under the early morning sun.

Conclusion

Whether you're looking for an adrenaline rush or a peaceful sunrise above the clouds, Bangalore's surrounding hills have something for everyone. Remember to carry a good torch, a jacket for the summit chill, and always trek with experienced guides for night trails.
