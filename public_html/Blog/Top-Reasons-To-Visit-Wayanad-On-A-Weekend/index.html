5 Reasons to Visit Wayanad This Weekend

By Hikers Horizon Team • May 2026 • 4 min read

Just a 6-hour drive from Bangalore lies Wayanad, the green paradise of Kerala. Nested among the mountains of the Western Ghats, it is known for its pristine beauty, spice plantations, and abundant wildlife.

If you haven't booked your weekend plans yet, here are the top 5 reasons you should pack your bags and head to Wayanad immediately.

1. The Heart-Shaped Lake at Chembra Peak

Chembra is the highest peak in Wayanad at 2,100 meters above sea level. Halfway up the trek, you will encounter *Hridaya Saras*, a naturally heart-shaped lake that is believed to have never dried up. The view of the surrounding valleys from the top is absolutely breathtaking.

2. The Ancient Edakkal Caves

Step back in time to the Neolithic age! The Edakkal Caves are natural rock formations adorned with ancient pictorial writings believed to be dating back to at least 6,000 BCE. The short trek to reach the caves offers stunning panoramas.

3. Majestic Waterfalls

Wayanad is home to some of South India's most stunning cascades:

  • Soochipara Falls: A massive three-tiered waterfall surrounded by dense forests. You can even swim in the pool below!
  • Meenmutty Falls: The largest waterfall in Wayanad, requiring an exciting 2km jungle trek to reach.

4. Tea Estates and Spice Gardens

Wayanad's climate is perfect for growing tea, coffee, cardamom, and pepper. Simply driving through the winding roads surrounded by endless acres of manicured tea estates is a therapeutic experience.

5. Wildlife Spotting at Muthanga

Part of the Nilgiri Biosphere Reserve, the Wayanad Wildlife Sanctuary is your best chance to spot wild elephants, deer, bison, and if you are extremely lucky, a tiger or a leopard in their natural habitat.
