Step-by-Step Guide: Kumara Parvatha (Pushpagiri) Trek Permit Booking on Aranya Vihara Portal

By Hikers Horizon Team • May 2026 • 5 min read

Rising to **5,627 feet** in the Coorg region of Karnataka, **Kumara Parvatha** (also known as the Pushpagiri Peak) is widely regarded as the **toughest and most challenging trek in South India**. With a demanding 22 km trail through dense jungle, steep slopes, and rocky terrain, it's the ultimate test of physical endurance.

Since the peak lies inside the strictly preserved **Pushpagiri Wildlife Sanctuary**, the Karnataka Forest Department requires all trekkers to secure an official **trekking permit online** prior to embarking on the trek.

All permits are managed through the official portal — **Aranya Vihara**. In this guide, we walk you through how to secure your permits step-by-step.

⚠️ Crucial Checklist Warning: Unlike other Karnataka treks, Kumara Parvatha permits require a strict plastic security deposit at the forest office checkpoint. Make sure to keep your plastic count exact to avoid heavy fines!

How to Book Kumara Parvatha Permits on Aranya Vihara Portal

To reserve your permit slots online, follow this detailed walkthrough:

  1. Visit the Portal: Go to the official Karnataka forest booking platform: aranyavihaara.karnataka.gov.in.
  2. Account Login: Create an account using your mobile number and authenticate the log-in via the OTP sent to your phone.
  3. Search Kumara Parvatha: On the trekking dashboard, select the **Madikeri (Coorg)** division or search for **"Kumara Parvatha"** (or Pushpagiri) in the main search field.
  4. Choose Date: Select your desired date for the trek.
  5. Add Trekkers: Input names, ages, genders, and upload soft copies of government-issued photo IDs (Aadhar Card, Passport, or DL) for all participants.
  6. Online Payment: Pay the online permit entry fee of **₹425 per trekker** (includes overnight permits & platform charges) using UPI, Net Banking, or Credit/Debit Card.
  7. Save the Permit: Download and print the PDF transaction confirmation page to present at the checkpost.

Essential Checkpoint Guidelines & Rules

The checkpoint officers are extremely strict about national park conservation guidelines:

  • Original ID Verification: Make sure to carry the exact physical original ID card that you uploaded during booking.
  • Strict Ban on single-use Plastics: All plastic packaging, including snack wrappers and disposable plastic water bottles, is checked at the gate. Bring a reusable flask or steel bottle.
  • Trek Timings: Treks must begin between 06:00 AM and 09:00 AM. Camping on the summit is strictly illegal; all trekkers must descend before sunset.
  • Safety Prep: The trail has steep gradients and high wind exposures near the ridge. Make sure you wear high-grip trekking shoes and carry rain gear.
