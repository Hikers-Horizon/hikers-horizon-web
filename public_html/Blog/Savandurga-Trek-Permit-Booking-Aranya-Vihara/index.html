Step-by-Step Guide: Savandurga Trek Permit Booking on Aranya Vihara Portal

By Hikers Horizon Team • May 2026 • 4 min read

Rising to **4,022 feet**, **Savandurga** is recognized as **Asia's largest monolith hill**. Situated roughly 60 km west of Bangalore, it is one of the most popular trekking destinations in Karnataka. The monolith comprises two hills: Karigudda (Black hill) and Biligudda (White hill). Biligudda is the designated trail for trekkers, offering a thrilling 50-degree steep climb on solid granite rock.

Because the monolith is situated within a sensitive forest range, the Karnataka Forest Department requires all trekkers to register and purchase **online trekking permits** beforehand.

Savandurga permits are managed through the official Karnataka Forest booking system — **Aranya Vihara**. In this guide, we will walk you through how to secure your permits step-by-step.

⚠️ Critical Granite Warning: Savandurga is a solid block of smooth granite. When it rains, the surface becomes extremely slippery and dangerous. Ensure you check local weather forecasts and wear shoes with excellent rubber grip!

How to Book Savandurga Permits on Aranya Vihara Portal

To reserve your permit slots online, follow this detailed walkthrough:

  1. Visit the Portal: Open the official Karnataka forest booking portal: aranyavihara.karnataka.gov.in.
  2. Log in / Sign Up: Create an account using your mobile number and log in using the OTP sent via SMS.
  3. Search Savandurga Trek: In the dashboard's trekking tab, search for **"Savandurga"** or look under the **Bengaluru Rural** division.
  4. Select Trek Date: Choose your date from the available slot calendar. There is a daily cap on slots, so weekends book out early.
  5. Enter Trekker Details: Enter the names, ages, and genders of all trekkers. You must upload a copy of a government-approved photo ID (like Aadhaar, Driving License, or Voter ID) for each participant.
  6. Complete Online Payment: Pay the entry fee of **₹250 per trekker** using UPI, Debit/Credit Card, or Net Banking.
  7. Save and Print Permit: Once successfully paid, download your permit PDF. Print a copy to present at the checkpost.

Important Checkpoint Rules & Advice

The local forest officers at the Savandurga base are highly active and enforce the following rules:

  • ID Match Check: Bring the original physical ID card that corresponds to the one uploaded during booking. Checkpost staff will verify details.
  • No Night Trekking: Unofficial night trekking is strictly prohibited. The forest entry gates open at 06:00 AM. Trespassing at night can attract heavy legal penalties.
  • Plastic ban: Single-use plastic bottles, snack wrappers, and trash are checked. Carry food in reusable containers and use reusable bottles.
  • Stay on Designated Trail: Follow the white arrow markers painted on the monolith surface. Deviating off the trail can lead to steep, slick drop-offs that are highly unsafe.
