The Ultimate Gokarna Beach Trek Itinerary from Bangalore

By Hikers Horizon Team • May 2026 • 6 min read

Goa is too crowded, and Pondicherry is too commercial. If you're looking for pristine beaches, laid-back cafes, and a true backpacker vibe, Gokarna is the ultimate coastal escape from Bangalore.

Unlike regular beach vacations where you just sit on the sand, the **Gokarna Beach Trek** involves hiking through tropical forests to cross over hills that separate five spectacular beaches.

The 5-Beach Trek Route

The famous Gokarna beach trek is roughly 10 kilometers long and takes you across rocky terrain and jungle paths. The traditional route goes from North to South:

1. Gokarna Main Beach

Start your journey here. It's the longest beach and is located close to the Mahabaleshwar Temple. From here, you hike up a small hill to reach the next destination.

2. Kudle Beach

Shaped like a perfect "C", Kudle is famous for its vibrant sunset views and numerous cafes. Grab a quick breakfast or a juice here before tackling the next hill.

3. Om Beach

The most famous beach in Gokarna, naturally shaped like the auspicious Om symbol (ॐ). It has rocky outcrops making it perfect for photography and relaxing.

4. Half Moon Beach

Separated by a dense jungle trek from Om beach, Half Moon is much smaller, less commercialized, and completely secluded. You cannot reach here by car.

5. Paradise Beach

The final stop. Living up to its name, this tiny strip of sand is surrounded by rocks and dense forest. It's the ultimate spot to end your trek and watch the waves.

Best Time to Visit

The ideal time for the Gokarna Beach Trek is between **October and March**. The weather is cool, the humidity is manageable, and the skies are clear. Avoid the monsoon season (June to September) as the trekking trails become dangerously slippery.

What to Pack

  • Lightweight cotton clothes and swimwear
  • Comfortable walking shoes (do not trek in flip-flops!)
  • Sunscreen, sunglasses, and a hat
  • A reusable water bottle
  • Power bank (electricity at Half Moon and Paradise can be unreliable)
