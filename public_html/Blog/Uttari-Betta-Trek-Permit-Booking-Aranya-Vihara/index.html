Step-by-Step Guide: Uttari Betta (Hutridurga) Trek Permit Booking on Aranya Vihara Portal

By Hikers Horizon Team • May 2026 • 4 min read

Situated around 75 km from Bangalore, **Uttari Betta** (also known as **Hutridurga**) is widely recognized as one of the finest and most beautiful sunrise trekking routes in Karnataka. Rising to **3,708 feet**, this historical trek takes you through seven levels of ancient fort walls built by Kempegowda, narrow stone passageways, and lush grassy fields, ending at a breathtaking summit shrine that offers a 360-degree vista of the surrounding countryside.

To preserve the rich natural beauty and historical ruins of the sanctuary, the Karnataka Forest Department requires all trekkers to secure an **official trekking permit online** prior to starting the trek.

All permits are managed through the official portal — **Aranya Vihara**. In this guide, we walk you through how to secure your permits step-by-step.

ℹ️ Quick Trail Vibe: Uttari Betta is a moderate and highly scenic climb. Perfect for beginners and families alike, it offers incredible photography opportunities, especially as the morning mist flows through the old fort doorways!

How to Book Uttari Betta Permits on Aranya Vihara Portal

To reserve your permit slots online, follow this detailed walkthrough:

  1. Visit the Portal: Open the official Karnataka forest booking platform: aranyavihara.karnataka.gov.in.
  2. Account Registration: Log in using your mobile number and authenticate via the OTP sent to your phone. If you are new to the platform, create a quick profile.
  3. Find Uttari Betta Trek: Navigate to the trekking menu and search for **"Uttari Betta"** (or Hutridurga) or look under the **Bengaluru Rural** or **Tumkur** division sections.
  4. Select Date: Pick your trekking date. Due to its popularity for weekend sunrise expeditions, booking at least 3-4 days in advance is highly recommended.
  5. Enter Participant Details: Enter the name, age, and gender of each trekker in your group. Upload a clear photograph or copy of a government-recognized photo ID (Aadhaar, Passport, or Voter ID).
  6. Complete Online Payment: Pay the official entrance permit fee of **₹250 per trekker** using UPI, Credit/Debit Cards, or Net Banking.
  7. Download Permit: Save the resulting PDF booking permit and print a copy to show forest guards at the starting gate.

Essential Checkpoint Rules & Tips

Forest department staff strictly monitor the trail to keep it clean and safe. Make sure you follow these rules:

  • Physical ID Verification: Make sure to carry the exact physical original ID card that you uploaded during booking.
  • Zero Littering: Hutridurga is a highly respected eco-tourism site. Forest department officials check backpacks for disposable plastics. Avoid bringing single-use plastic wrappers or bags, and carry a reusable water bottle.
  • Trek Timings: The official forest gate opens at 06:00 AM. Night trekking without authorization is illegal and highly dangerous due to steep drops.
  • Footwear: Though the trek is relatively easy, certain rocky stretches and ancient stone steps are smooth and can be slick with morning dew. Wear proper high-grip sports or trekking shoes.
