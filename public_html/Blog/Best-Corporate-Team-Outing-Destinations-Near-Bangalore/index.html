Best Corporate Team Outing Destinations Near Bangalore

By Hikers Horizon Team • May 2026 • 5 min read

A strong team is built outside the boardroom. While luxury resorts are great for relaxation, nothing bonds a team quite like shared adventure, overcoming physical challenges, and experiencing the great outdoors together.

Here are the top three destinations near Bangalore that offer the perfect mix of nature, adventure, and team-building potential.

1. Dandeli: The Adrenaline Hub

If your team loves high-energy activities, Dandeli is the unmatched champion. Located in North Karnataka, the Kali River provides the perfect playground for water sports.

  • White Water Rafting: The ultimate teamwork exercise. Navigating the rapids of the Kali River requires synchrony and communication.
  • Jungle Safari: Take a break from the water and explore the Dandeli Wildlife Sanctuary.
  • Kayaking & Ziplining: Perfect for overcoming personal fears and building confidence.

2. Coorg: The Scotland of India

For teams looking for a blend of luxury, cool weather, and moderate adventure, Coorg is the classic choice. It's ideal for a two-day retreat.

  • Tadiandamol Trek: Conquer the highest peak in Coorg together as a team. The hike is moderate but highly rewarding.
  • Coffee Estate Walks: Unwind and network while walking through sprawling aromatic coffee plantations.
  • Campfire Bonding: End the day with a BBQ and music under the stars, away from city lights.

3. Chikmagalur: Coffee & Clouds

Located at the foothills of the Mullayanagiri range, Chikmagalur offers misty mornings and excellent trekking trails.

  • Mullayanagiri Trek: Hike to the highest peak in Karnataka. The wind at the top is exhilarating and makes for an incredible team photo.
  • Hebbe Falls Jeep Ride: A thrilling off-road jeep ride through private estates to reach the majestic Hebbe Falls.
