Step-by-Step Guide: Skandagiri Trek Permit Booking on Aranya Vihara Portal

By Hikers Horizon Team • May 2026 • 5 min read

If you're an adventure enthusiast living in Bangalore, the **Skandagiri Sunrise Trek** is almost certainly on your weekend bucket list. Located around 62 km from the city near Chikkaballapur, this 4,757 ft hill offers a spectacular "above the clouds" sunrise experience that is unmatched in South India.

However, because Skandagiri falls within a protected forest zone, the **Karnataka Forest Department** strictly regulates entry. You cannot simply walk in; you **must book an official trekking permit online** prior to your journey.

Recently, the booking system transitioned to the new official forest portal — **Aranya Vihara**. In this guide, we walk you through the entire step-by-step process of booking your Skandagiri permit securely online.

⚠️ Crucial Warning: Slots are extremely limited (only a few hundred per day) and the highly popular sunrise slots sell out within minutes of opening. Booking in advance is highly recommended!

How to Book Skandagiri Permits on Aranya Vihara Portal

Follow these exact steps to reserve your trekking permit:

  1. Visit the Official Portal: Open your browser and navigate to the official Karnataka Eco-Tourism portal: aranyavihara.karnataka.gov.in.
  2. Create an Account: Register your account using your active mobile number. Enter the OTP sent to your phone to complete your login.
  3. Navigate to Trekking: On the home dashboard, select the "Trekking" category. Search or filter the ranges for Chikkaballapur or enter "Skandagiri" (also referred to as Kalavara Durga) in the search bar.
  4. Select Date & Slot: Choose your desired date. You will be presented with two main slots:
    • Sunrise Slot (Night Trek): Starts around 03:30 AM (Highly recommended to catch the cloud bed at dawn).
    • Day Slot (Morning Trek): Starts around 07:30 AM.
  5. Input Trekker Details: Enter the names, ages, and genders of all participants. You will need to upload a soft copy of a government-approved photo ID (like Aadhar Card, Driving License, or Voter ID) for each trekker.
  6. Make Payment: Complete the transaction securely online via UPI, Net Banking, or Credit/Debit Card. The standard permit entry fee is **₹250 per person** (plus gateway taxes).
  7. Download Permit: Once the payment is verified, download and take a physical printout of the generated PDF permit.

Essential Rules & Things to Carry

The forest checkpoint staff are extremely strict about conservation. Keep these rules in mind to avoid being turned back:

  • Original ID Proof: You must carry the same original physical ID card that you uploaded during online booking. Copies on phones are often rejected.
  • Plastic and Eco-Rules: Single-use plastic bottles, snack wrappers, and polythene are strictly banned. Bags are thoroughly inspected at the entry gates.
  • Prohibited Items: Alcohol, smoking, and Bluetooth speakers are completely prohibited inside the forest reserve.
  • Footwear: Ensure you wear high-grip trekking shoes. The initial trail consists of steep granite boulders that can be extremely slippery in the dark.
