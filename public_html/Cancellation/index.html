1. Non-Refundables

  • 25% booking deposit (Processing fee)
  • Forest permits and government entry fees
  • Special equipment rentals if already reserved
  • Third-party transportation service charges
  • Banking and transaction gateway fees (2-5%)

2. Special Cases

  • Medical Emergencies: Full credit voucher provided upon submission of a valid medical certificate within 3 days.
  • Weather Cancellations: If a trek is cancelled by us due to safety concerns, full credit voucher or 50% refund is offered.
  • No Shows: Failure to arrive at the designated meeting point results in 100% forfeiture.

3. Refund Process

  • Processing time: 7-21 working days from approval.
  • Method: Refunded to the original bank account or source of payment.
  • Credit vouchers: Issued immediately upon approval.
  • Disputes: Must be raised within 48 hours of original trek date.
