Conquer the peaks, explore historical ruins, and soak in the majestic sunrise from above the clouds

Skandagiri Trek Gallery

About The Trek

LocationSkandagiri, Bangalore
Start Time11:30 PM
End Time12:30 PM
Duration13 hours (approx.)
Height4,757 ft.
Distance4 km
DifficultyModerate

Itinerary

  • 11:30 PM - Pick up from Bengaluru
  • 03:30 AM - Reach Skandagiri Base point
  • 04:00 AM - Start trek to Skandagiri Hill and Rappelling, 8km up and down
  • 09:30 AM - Breakfast at Breakfast point
  • 10:30 AM - Start back to Bangalore
  • 12:30 PM - Reach back to Bangalore

Pickup Points

  • 11:00 PM - Gopalan Arcade Mall RR Nagar
  • 11:15 PM - Kathriguppe Circle
  • 11:35 PM - Banashankari Bus Stop
  • 11:50 PM - BTM Udupi Garden
  • 12:00 AM - SilkBoard Bust Stop
  • 12:30 AM - Bellanduru Near Shell Petrol Pump
  • 01:00 AM - Kalamandir Marathahalli
  • 01:20 AM - Tin Factory near HP patrol Pump
  • 01:40 AM - Hebbal opposite Esteem Mal

Know Before You Go

  • Timings for meals will be specific and informed by the guide
  • Basic level of fitness is compulsory
  • Follow instructor guidelines carefully
  • Avoid using earphones during trek
  • Timings account for Bangalore traffic
  • No valuables or expensive jewelry
  • Alcohol and intoxicants prohibited

Things to Carry

  • Backpack
  • Raincoat
  • Water Bottles
  • Energy Food
  • Personal Medication
  • Sweater
  • Extra Clothing
  • Trekking Shoes
  • Flashlight
  • Camera (optional)
