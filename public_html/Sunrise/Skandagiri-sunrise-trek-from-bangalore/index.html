Skandagiri Sunrise Trek

Conquer the clouds at 4,757 ft. An iconic night trek journey from Bangalore.

Skandagiri Sunrise Trek Overview

Difficulty Moderate
Altitude 4,757 ft.
Distance 4 km Ascent
Start Time 11:30 PM

Experience one of the most celebrated night treks near Bangalore. Skandagiri (Kalavara Durga) offers a unique trail that transitions from dense forests to historical fort ruins, ultimately leading to a summit where you stand above a literal sea of clouds. Perfect for adventure enthusiasts looking for that magical golden hour.

Discover the history of Skandagiri’s ancient fort ruins with insights from Karnataka Tourism.
