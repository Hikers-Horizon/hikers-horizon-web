Uttaribetta Sunrise Trek Overview

Uttaribetta is officially recognized as one of the best trekking trails near Bangalore. The trek provides a stunning journey through seven levels of fort walls, ancient stone-carved steps, and lush vegetation, leading to a peak that offers a 360-degree view of the vast green landscape.

Learn more about the scenic trails of Kolar and Magadi with Karnataka Tourism.

Important Information

Trek Guidelines & Rules

  • • Uttaribetta is a clean-trek zone; absolute zero-litter policy
  • • Gateways are narrow; avoid pushing during the ascent
  • • The stone steps can be slippery when misty
  • • Respect the local temple priest if encountered at the peak
  • • Maintain group pace; do not wander off into forest patches
