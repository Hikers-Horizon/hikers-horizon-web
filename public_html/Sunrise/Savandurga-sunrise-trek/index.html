Savandurga Monolith Trek

Climb Asia's largest monolith. A challenging 4,022 ft journey through history.

Adventure Overview

Difficulty Moderate - Hard
Altitude 4,022 ft.
Monolith Asia's Largest
Start Time 11:30 PM

Savandurga is a hill 60 km west of Bangalore, India. The hill is considered to be among the largest monolith hills in Asia. This trek offers a steep climb on a granite rock surface, leading to ancient fort ruins and a Nandi temple at the peak with panoramic views of the Arkavathi river.

Explore the natural heritage of Savandurga with Karnataka Tourism.

Know Before You Go

  • • Steep granite surface requires good trekking shoes
  • • Stay on designated trails to avoid slippery zones
  • • High winds at the peak; carry a light jacket
  • • Avoid night descent without professional guides
  • • Respect the ancient temple premises at the summit
