What do travelers with great taste do beyond the beaches? They embark on the legendary Dudhsagar Falls Trek & Dandeli Adventure. Dudhsagar Falls is a majestic four-tiered waterfall on the Mandovi River along the Goa-Karnataka border, surrounded by deep deciduous jungle and the iconic railway bridge.

On Day 1, we trek to the magnificent roaring Dudhsagar Waterfalls and take a refreshing dip with safety life jackets. On Day 2, we head into the wild heart of Dandeli for high-energy water activities (white water rafting, kayaking, natural jacuzzi) followed by the serene views of the Supa Dam Backwaters.

Explore the rocky trails of Karnataka with Karnataka Tourism.

Day 1 Exploration & Adventure at Dudhsagar Falls Trek

07:00 AM – 08:00 AM: Arrival at Campsite, check-in, have breakfast, explore campsite, carry packed lunch.

08:00 AM – 09:30 AM: Head out to and reach the starting point of the Dudhsagar falls trek.

09:30 AM – 01:30 PM: Start the trek and reach the waterfalls (4 hours of scenic Western Ghats trails).

Note: All trekkers must acquire life jackets from the designated department before the trek if they want to jump into the water.

01:30 PM – 02:30 PM: Have packed lunch and take a refreshing dip in the natural pool below the roaring falls.

02:30 PM: Trek back down and get back to the campsite for hot dinner and rest.

Note: This is the primary trek for the day. The timings for this trek change slightly with each group.

Day 2 Dandeli Adventure & Supa Dam Backwaters!

08:15 AM: Freshen up, have breakfast, check out, and get ready to travel to Dandeli.

08:15 AM – 09:30 AM: Head to Dandeli Water Activities Area and reach there.

Note: Activities in Dandeli are subject to river conditions and forest availability. If activities are closed or unavailable, we will return to Bengaluru.

09:30 AM – 02:30 PM: Spend time at the Water Activities Area (White Water Rafting, Kayaking, Coracle Ride, Natural Jacuzzi Bath) *Self-Sponsored.

02:30 PM – 03:30 PM: Have delicious Lunch *Self-Sponsored.

04:00 PM – 06:00 PM: Head to Supa Dam Backwater and witness the serene sunset reflections over the reservoir.

06:00 PM: Head towards Bengaluru, stop for Dinner en route *Self-Sponsored.

Day 3 Homeward Bound — Arrive in Bengaluru

06:00 AM – Reach Bangalore: After an overnight journey, arrive back in Bangalore feeling refreshed from the trip.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • How hard is the Dudhsagar Falls Trek?

    The trek difficulty is Moderate to Difficult. It covers approximately 11-14 km through rocky tracks, stream crossings, and jungle trails. Trekkers should have basic fitness.

  • Is swimming required to enjoy the waterfalls and Dandeli?

    No, swimming is not mandatory. Certified life jackets are mandatory and provided for entering the water at Dudhsagar, and trained safety kayakers supervise water activities in Dandeli.

  • Are water activities included in Dandeli?

    Transport to Dandeli water area and Supa Dam is fully included. Individual activities such as white water rafting, kayaking, and natural jacuzzi bath are self-sponsored so travelers can choose what they prefer.
