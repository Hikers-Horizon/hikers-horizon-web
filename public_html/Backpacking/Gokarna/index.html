Unwind by the Arabian Sea, explore mystical limestone caverns, and discover coastal Karnataka’s most breathtaking natural wonders with the official 3-Day Gokarna, Honnavar & Murudeshwar Backpacking Tour by Hikers Horizon.

On Day 1, we conquer the famous five beaches of Gokarna (Belekan, Paradise, Half Moon, Om, and Kudle Beach), witness a soul-stirring coastal sunset, browse the lively Gokarna flea market, and relax by a beachfront campfire. On Day 2, we journey inland to the lush forest oasis of Vibhooti Falls for a refreshing swim, explore the towering natural monoliths of Yana Caves, marvel at the ramparts of Mirjan Fort, and seek blessings at the sacred Mahabaleshwar Temple. On Day 3, we stand in awe of the towering 123-foot Shiva Statue and Murudeshwar Temple, walk through the serene Sharavathi Kandla Mangrove Boardwalk, and glide along the tranquil Honnavara Backwaters (with Jog Falls as a scenic contingency during bad weather).

Trip Schedule

Detailed Trip Itinerary

Day 1 Embrace the Natural Beauty of Gokarna’s Beaches

• 9:00 AM – 10:00 AM: Arrival at Campsite and Get Ready

• 10:00 AM – 10:30 AM: Breakfast and Campsite Exploration

• 10:30 AM – 11:00 AM: Depart to and reach Belekan Beach, the starting point of our trek

• 11:00 AM – 11:30 AM: Trek from Belekan Beach to Paradise Beach (30 mins)

• 11:30 AM – 12:30 PM: Enjoy Paradise Beach

• 12:30 PM – 01:30 PM: Trek to and reach Half-Moon Beach (1 hour)

• 1:30 PM – 02:30 PM: Relax at Half-Moon Beach

• 2:30 PM – 03:30 PM: Trek to and reach Om Beach (1 hour)

• 3:30 PM – 04:00 PM: Enjoy Om Beach

• 4:00 PM – 04:30 PM: Lunch*Self-Sponsored

• 4:30 PM – 05:00 PM: Trek through the Forest to Kudle Beach (30 mins)

• 05:00 PM – 06:30 PM: Sunset at Kudle Beach - Arrive at Kudle Beach in time to catch a mesmerizing sunset.

• 6:30 PM – 08:30 PM: Trek to reach the stay on Gokarna Beach while simultaneously exploring the Gokarna Market (2 hours)

Note: Please take a private transport to reach the stay if you are unable to trek back to the Gokarna Beach, which would take around 1.5 hours to reach.

• 08:30 PM: Head back to the campsite and unwind.

• 09:30 PM - 10:30 PM: Campfire and Dinner - Return to campsite, enjoy the warmth of a campfire, share stories with fellow travelers, have dinner, and then retire for the night.

Day 2 From Cascading Falls to Historic Forts

Day 2: From Cascading Falls to Historic Forts

• 5:00 a.m. – Departure for Vibhooti Falls: Early departure to the enchanting Vibhooti Falls.

• 10:30 a.m. – Explore Vibhooti Falls: Enjoy the serene ambiance and optional swimming.

• 12:00 p.m. – Journey to Yana Caves: Visit the mystical Yana Caves, known for their unique rock formations.

• 2:00 p.m. – Lunch at a Local Restaurant (Self-sponsored): Enjoy local cuisine.

• 3:00 p.m. – Visit Mirjan Fort: Explore the historical architecture of Mirjan Fort.

• 5:30 p.m. – Visit Mahabaleshwar Temple: Experience spiritual moments at the Gokarna Temple.

* NOTE: Dress Code is generally Traditional Attire for Mahabaleshwar Temple i.e. For Men - Dhotis or Mundus & for Women - sarees, salwar kameez, or other traditional outfits that cover their shoulders and knees. Clothing like jeans, shorts, and revealing attire are not permitted.

• 7:00 p.m. – Return and Rest: Return to your accommodation, have dinner and rest for the night.

Day 3 Spiritual and Natural Wonders

• 07:00 AM – 08:00 AM: Freshen up, have breakfast and checkout of homestay

• 10:00 AM – 12:30 PM: Visit Murudeshwar temple and Shiva Statue

* NOTE: Dress Code is generally Traditional Attire for Murudeshwar Temple i.e. For Men - Dhotis or Mundus & for Women - sarees, salwar kameez, or other traditional outfits that cover their shoulders and knees. Clothing like jeans, shorts, and revealing attire are not permitted.

• 01:00 PM – 02:30 PM: Enjoy a peaceful walk in the Mangrove Boardwalk

• 02:30 PM – 03:30 PM: Have Lunch (*Self Sponsored)

• 04:00 PM – 06:00 PM: Enjoy Boating at Honnavara Backwaters i.e. if venue is open (Venue closes if there is bad weather). In case of venue closure, Visit the mighty Jog Falls.

• 06:30 PM: Head back to Bangalore

• 09:00 PM: Stop for dinner on the way (*Self Sponsored)

Day 4 Homeward Bound

• 05:00 AM – Reach Bangalore

Drop-off Points: The drop-off points will be the same as the pick-up points to ensure convenience for all travelers. Please ensure you have all your belongings before leaving the vehicle.

Home Sweet Home:
Once you’ve reached your drop-off point, it’s time to head home, take a well-deserved rest, and start planning your next adventure!
