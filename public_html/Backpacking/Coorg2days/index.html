Coorg, or Kodagu, is a rural district in the southwest Indian state of Karnataka. Known as the 'Scotland of India,' it is famous for its coffee estates, waterfalls, and brave Kodava culture. This 2-day trip is designed to give you a perfect snapshot of Coorg's royal heritage and natural splendor.

Learn about the Kodava traditions from Karnataka Tourism.

Coorg 2-Day Trek Itinerary

Day 1 Falls & Royal Sunsets

Arrival in Madikeri and check-in. Visit the roaring Abbey Falls tucked inside coffee plantations. Explore the unique Omkareshwara Temple (Islamic-Gothic fusion). Witness a glorious sunset at Raja's Seat, the seat of the Kodagu Kings.

Day 2 Estate Walks & Heritage

Early morning walked through a private coffee estate. Visit the Madikeri Fort and museum. Head to Dubare Elephant Camp for some riverside peace. Late afternoon return journey to Bangalore.

Coorg 2-Day Trek Guidelines

  • • Coorg is rainy; always carry an umbrella or raincoat
  • • Abbey falls involves a short downhill walk (paved)
  • • Elephant camp activities are subject to forest rules
  • • Try local Pandi Curry (for meat eaters) or Akki Roti
  • • Madikeri stays can get quite cold at night
