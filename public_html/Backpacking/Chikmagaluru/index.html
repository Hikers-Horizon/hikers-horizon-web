Trail Overview & Experience

Chikmagalur, known as the 'Coffee Land of Karnataka,' offers a refreshing escape into the Western Ghats. From the mist-covered summit of Mullayanagiri to the hidden Jhari Waterfalls and the aromatic coffee trails, this trip is designed for those looking to reconnect with nature.

Trip Schedule

Detailed Trip Itinerary

Day 1 Peak Vistas & Estate Vibes
  • Arrive in Chikmagalur, check-in to estate homestay. Head to Mullayanagiri (Karnataka's highest peak) for misty views. Visit Seethalayanagiri and Baba Budangiri. Sunset at a scenic viewpoint.
Day 2 Waterfalls & Coffee Trails
  • Visit the hidden Jhari Waterfalls (Buttermilk Falls). Walk through a private coffee estate and learn about coffee processing. Optional visit to Belur temple. Head back to Bangalore.

Trail Safety

Important Guidelines & Rules

  • • Weather changes rapidly at peaks; carry a windcheater
  • • Estate homestays offer a unique local experience
  • • Jhari falls access may involve a local jeep ride (seasonal)
  • • Respect the estate boundaries while walking
  • • Carry warm clothing for peaceful but cool nights
