The Highland Expedition

Into Kerala’s Misty Mountains

Looking for a weekend that blends adrenaline, scenic peaks, and Kerala’s rich highland heritage? The Munnar & Kolukkumalai Adventure is an exhilarating 2-day getaway into the cloud-kissed landscapes of the Western Ghats.

From witnessing the legendary dawn above the clouds at Kolukkumalai (7,130 FT)—accessible only via rugged 4x4 mountain jeeps—to hiking through blooming tea terraces, watching ancient Kalaripayattu martial arts, and exploring thunderous waterfalls like Attukad and Periyakanal, this tour is curated for nature lovers and adventurers alike.

🚙

Kolukkumalai Sunrise 4x4 Jeep Safari & Weather Advisory

Kolukkumalai sunrise point is situated at 7,130 FT and reached via an exhilarating off-road trail regulated by the local Jeep Union (self-sponsored, split among group members). Morning temperatures often drop below 10°C—make sure to pack a warm jacket, fleece, and beanie!

Trip Schedule

2-Day Mountain Itinerary

Day 0 Overnight Journey from Bangalore

Depart Bangalore on an overnight scenic journey through the rolling plains and Ghats towards Munnar.

  • 08:00 PM: Boarding begins across our designated Bangalore pickup points (Banashankari, BTM, Silk Board, Koramangala, Domlur, MG Road, Mekhri Circle, Yeshwanthpur).
  • 10:30 PM: Highway comfort stop; overnight road trip into God's Own Country.
Day 1 Tea Estates, Waterfalls & Kalaripayattu Culture
  • 08:00 AM: Arrive at our Munnar hillside campsite, freshen up, and enjoy a traditional Kerala breakfast.
  • 10:00 AM: Visit Panthom Hill viewpoint and the dramatic Jaguar Rock vantage.
  • 11:30 AM: Guided tour of a century-old Tea Factory and local Chocolate Factory to taste fresh handcrafted blends.
  • 01:30 PM: Self-sponsored lunch at Munnar town featuring authentic Malabar & Kerala specialties.
  • 03:30 PM: Hike to the roaring cascades of Attukad Waterfall and capture scenic views of Lockhart Gap.
  • 05:30 PM: Attend a live, heart-pounding Kalaripayattu & Kathakali cultural show at Kalari Kshetra (*Self-sponsored ticket).
  • 08:00 PM: Return to campsite. Savor a hot dinner, gather around the crackling campfire, and rest for the early morning summit.
Day 2 Kolukkumalai Sunrise Above Clouds & Return
  • 04:00 AM: Early morning departure in 4x4 rugged jeeps towards Kolukkumalai (7,130 FT).
  • 06:00 AM: Witness one of Asia's most breathtaking sunrises, with crimson light breaking through a blanket of white clouds below you.
  • 08:30 AM: Visit Singapara & Signal Point; savor hot tea brewed right from the highest tea gardens in the world.
  • 10:30 AM: Return to campsite for breakfast, pack bags, and check out.
  • 12:30 PM: Visit Mattupetty Dam and echo point. Optional boating / kayaking.
  • 02:30 PM: Lunch break (*Self-sponsored).
  • 06:00 PM: Board our vehicle and depart back to Bangalore with lifelong mountain memories.
  • 05:00 AM (Day 3): Reach Bangalore; drop-off at your original pickup locations.

Common Queries

Frequently Asked Questions

How cold does it get at Kolukkumalai sunrise?

At 7,130 FT before dawn, temperatures can dip between 6°C to 12°C depending on the season, with gusty mountain winds. Warm jackets, thermals, and windcheaters are strongly advised.

How does the Kolukkumalai Jeep Safari work?

Due to rugged forest terrain, private transport cannot climb Kolukkumalai. Local 4x4 jeeps are hired from the base union (self-sponsored, typically ₹2,500 - ₹3,000 per jeep shared among 6-7 trekkers). Our trek captain coordinates the entire convoy smoothly.

Is this trip suitable for solo travelers and girls?

Yes, 100%! Over 60% of our travelers join solo. We ensure dedicated female-friendly accommodations, experienced trek captains, and a welcoming community atmosphere.
