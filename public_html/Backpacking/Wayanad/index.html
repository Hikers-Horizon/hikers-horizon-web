Wayanad Trek Overview

Vibe Highlands & Nature
Duration 2 Days / 1 Night

Wayanad is one of Kerala's most beautiful and diverse regions. From the ancient Edakkal Caves to the stunning Soochipara Waterfalls and the tranquil Banasura Sagar Dam, this backpacking trip offers an immersive experience into the wild and scenic beauty of the Western Ghats.

Day 0 Overnight Bangalore to Wayanad Trip

Pickup Points & Travel Details

Escape into the lush green hills of Wayanad with our hassle-free overnight journey from Bangalore. Enjoy a scenic road trip with private transportation and multiple convenient pickup points.

  • 11:30 PM: Pickup at Tin Factory

Day 1 Zipline your way to Happiness

09:00 AM - 10:00 AM: Reach homestay, freshen up and have breakfast

10:00 AM - 12:00 PM: Visit Soochipara Waterfalls, spend some quality time (Changing rooms are available).

12:00 PM - 03:30 PM: Lunch (Self Sponsored) and visit 900 Kandi Glass Bridge (Jeep ride is Self Sponsored).

04:00 PM - 06:00 PM: Engage in exhilarating activities such as ziplining, sky cycling, and giant swing (Self-Sponsored). Enjoy the Longest Zipline in Wayanad!

06:30 PM: Head back to homestay and have dinner.

Day 2 Dam and Cave Exploration!

07:00 AM - 08:30 AM: Freshen up, have breakfast, checkout and leave to Banasura Dam.

10:00 AM - 12:00 PM: Enjoy the scenic Banasura Dam (Activities are Self Sponsored).

12:00 PM - 03:00 PM: Head to Edakkal Caves, have lunch (Self Sponsored) on the way.

03:00 PM - 05:00 PM: Cave exploration - Edakkal Caves also known as "Ambukutty Mala" (translates to "arrow struck mountain").

05:00 PM: Board vehicle to reach Bengaluru.

10:00 PM: Reach Bengaluru. The drop-off points will be the same as the pick-up points.

Wayanad Trek Guidelines

  • • Waterfalls may be closed during heavy monsoons; check with us
  • • Edakkal Caves involve a steep climb; wear good shoes
  • • Leeches are common in damp areas; wear long socks
  • • Alcohol and smoking are prohibited during group treks
  • • Cooperate with local authorities at the dam and forest zones
